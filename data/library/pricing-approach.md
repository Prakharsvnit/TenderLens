# Pricing Approach

> Cobalt Harbor IT Services is a fictional company created for the TenderLens demo.

## Structure

- **Fixed monthly fee** for ongoing managed services and preventive maintenance, priced per user, with servers and network devices included.
- **Hourly rates** for on-demand calls and project work outside the agreed scope: $145 per hour for engineers, $175 per hour for senior engineers and architects, and 1.5 times those rates after hours.
- **Licences and equipment** are purchased directly by the client; we add no markup and no resale margin.

## Additional charges

Travel within 50 miles of one of our offices is included. Travel beyond 50 miles is billed at the IRS mileage rate. There are no setup fees for standard onboarding.

## Quotes

A client-specific monthly fee is quoted after the pre-bid walkthrough or discovery call. Proposals and cost schedules remain valid for 180 days.
