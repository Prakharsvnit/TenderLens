# Cobalt Harbor IT Services — Company Profile

> Cobalt Harbor IT Services is a fictional company created for the TenderLens demo. All names, numbers and references are invented.

## Overview

Cobalt Harbor IT Services, Inc. is a managed IT services provider founded in 2011 and headquartered in Atlanta, Georgia. We have delivered fully outsourced managed IT services to public-sector clients since 2013.

## Size and clients

- Full-time employees: 84 (technical support 52, consulting 9, project management 6, sales support 7, administrative support 10).
- Active clients: 61, of which 14 are public-sector organisations (municipalities, utility authorities and special districts).
- Endpoints under management: about 4,300.

## Offices

- Headquarters and network operations centre: Atlanta, Georgia.
- Florida field office: Tampa, Florida, opened in 2024 with six staff, including two field technicians. This office would service a Central Florida account.

## Registrations and standing

- Registered with the Georgia Secretary of State and with the Florida Division of Corporations as a foreign for-profit corporation.
- We obtain the local business licence or business tax receipt required by each client jurisdiction upon contract award.
- Cobalt Harbor is not listed in the SAM.gov exclusions (suspension and debarment) system; a current search printout is kept on file and supplied with proposals.
- No contract has been terminated for default or poor performance in the past five years.
- We maintain a drug-free workplace program and have been enrolled in E-Verify since 2016.

## Why public-sector clients choose us

We act as the single point of contact for all IT matters, explain technical issues in plain language for staff of every technical level, and take full operational responsibility for infrastructure, cloud, security operations and vendor coordination.
