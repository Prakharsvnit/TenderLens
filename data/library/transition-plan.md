# Transition and Onboarding Plan

> Cobalt Harbor IT Services is a fictional company created for the TenderLens demo.

## Days 0 to 30: discover and document

Knowledge transfer from the outgoing provider, credential and licence handover into the client's name, network and asset discovery, and documentation of every system. By day 30 we deliver a written assessment report covering hardware, software, licensing, peripherals, system performance, equipment life expectancy and capacity, security posture and current processes, with prioritised recommendations and cost estimates on request. The assessment is repeated annually.

## Days 31 to 60: stabilise

Monitoring, backup and patching brought to our standards; quick-win fixes; help desk fully live.

## Days 61 to 90: optimise

Roadmap agreed with the client, the first quarterly business review, and the one-year IT budget prepared.

## Records

We retain service records for seven years and give the client and its auditors access on request.
