# Service Levels and Support Model

> Cobalt Harbor IT Services is a fictional company created for the TenderLens demo.

## Availability

Our service desk and network operations centre are staffed 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, 365 days a year. Clients reach us by a toll-free phone number, by email, or through our service request portal, to which client staff have full access.

## Response and resolution targets

| Priority | Example | Response | Resolution target |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| P1 Emergency | Site down, security incident | 15 minutes, 24x7 | 4 hours |
| P2 High | Department or server degraded | 1 hour | 8 hours |
| P3 Normal | Single user issue | 4 hours | 2 business days |
| P4 Request | New user, change request | Next business day | 5 business days |

Emergency requests receive a technician response within 15 minutes at any hour, well inside a two-hour maximum.

## On-site support

- Georgia metro clients: on-site within 4 hours for P1, and weekly scheduled visits.
- Florida clients: technicians travel from our Tampa office. Scheduled preventive maintenance visits are monthly. On-demand on-site response is next business day, or same day for P1 incidents when a technician is available.

## Staffing

18 service desk analysts and 9 level-2 and level-3 engineers support all clients, with a named service delivery manager for each account.

## Escalation and authority

Escalation runs from level 1 to level 2, then to the service desk manager, the account's service delivery manager and our chief operating officer. The client's designated representative has final authority on conflicts.

## Maintenance windows and reporting

Maintenance is scheduled after hours or at weekends to avoid disrupting staff. Clients receive a monthly report on SLA attainment, ticket trends, patch compliance and backup status. Our 2025 SLA attainment across all clients was 98.6%.

## Charges

Ongoing preventive maintenance and support are covered by a fixed monthly fee. On-demand and project work outside the agreed scope is billed hourly; see the pricing approach.
