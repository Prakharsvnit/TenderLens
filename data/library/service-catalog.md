# Managed Services Catalogue

> Cobalt Harbor IT Services is a fictional company created for the TenderLens demo.

## Infrastructure and server administration

Server and workstation administration, capacity and performance monitoring, configuration and change management, patching and upgrades, and preventive maintenance with documented procedures. We maintain an up-to-date inventory of all hardware and software and share it with the client on request.

## Network administration

Management of firewalls, switches, routers, site-to-site and local wireless, WAN and LAN, VoIP phone systems, IP security cameras and public access cameras. We coordinate with broadband and internet service providers, perform minor cabling, monitor bandwidth and performance thresholds, and send alert notifications to designated client staff when a failure occurs.

## Cloud services

Administration of Microsoft 365 commercial tenants, Entra ID, and Microsoft Azure workloads, including identity and access management, user provisioning, virtual environments and cloud resource allocation.

## Email

Microsoft 365 email administration, domain configuration, mailbox lifecycle, encrypted email, email archiving and spam protection, all monitored by our team.

## Desktop and end-user support

Installation and configuration of desktops, laptops, mobile devices, printers, scanners and peripherals; diagnosis of desktop application and hardware issues; coordination with line-of-business application vendors.

## Backup and disaster recovery

Image-based server backup with immutable cloud copies, daily backup verification, quarterly restore tests and a documented disaster recovery runbook for each client.

## Vendor liaison and procurement as a service

We are the single point of contact for third-party vendors and application-specific support providers. Procurement as a service means we source quotes and manage orders, but every purchase is quoted in writing and approved in writing by the client before it is placed. Licences, subscriptions and equipment are always purchased in the client's name; we never bundle, resell or retain ownership of client licences. The client remains the named Microsoft tenant owner and keeps Global Administrator access at all times.

## Strategic planning (vCIO)

Annual IT strategy and roadmap, technology recommendations, design of major server, storage, backup and network upgrades, and support for a one-year IT budget, including end-of-life planning and identification of state and federal grant opportunities.

## Project consulting

Software installation, migrations and short- and long-range IT planning, quoted separately from the managed services fee.
