# Security and Compliance Overview

> Cobalt Harbor IT Services is a fictional company created for the TenderLens demo.

## Frameworks and attestations

- SOC 2 Type II report for our managed services, issued November 2025.
- Our security program is aligned to the NIST Cybersecurity Framework (CSF) 2.0.
- We have not completed an independent assessment against NIST SP 800-53 controls.

## CJIS

Cobalt Harbor has not supported environments subject to the FBI CJIS Security Policy. Our technicians have not completed CJIS Security Awareness Training at Level 4, and our staff have not undergone FDLE fingerprint-based background checks. Where a client needs CJIS-regulated systems supported, we would work with a CJIS-experienced teaming partner.

## Microsoft government clouds

We manage Microsoft 365 commercial tenants and two Azure Government subscriptions. We have not deployed or managed Microsoft 365 GCC High tenants.

## Security operations

- Multi-factor authentication for all administrative access.
- Managed endpoint detection and response on every server, desktop and laptop, plus managed antivirus and firewall policies.
- A SIEM monitored 24x7 by our NOC, with network intrusion detection and immediate notification to designated client staff of any suspected breach.
- Data loss prevention through Microsoft Purview policies.
- SOAR automation is limited to phishing triage playbooks today.

## Testing and training

- Annual external penetration test by an independent third party, and an annual risk assessment, for every managed client.
- Annual security awareness training for client staff, with monthly phishing simulations covering phishing and ransomware.
- Security audits on request.

## People and confidentiality

All employees pass a state criminal background check at hire and sign a confidentiality agreement. We consent to client-run criminal background investigations of any employee who has access to client systems, and provide the requested employee information.

## Remote access

Client remote access is delivered through a secure, MFA-protected gateway administered by our team at the client's request.
