# Insurance Coverage Summary

> Cobalt Harbor IT Services is a fictional company created for the TenderLens demo.

Coverage in force for the 2026 policy year, as stated on our certificate of insurance.

| Coverage | Limit |
| --- | --- |
| Commercial general liability | $2,000,000 per occurrence; $4,000,000 aggregate |
| Professional liability (errors and omissions) | $2,000,000 per occurrence and aggregate |
| Cyber liability and network security | $500,000 per occurrence and aggregate |
| Workers' compensation | Statutory limits |
| Commercial auto | $1,000,000 combined single limit |

The cyber liability policy covers data breach response, unauthorised access, ransomware, cyber extortion, privacy liability, regulatory defence, notification costs and system restoration, including acts of our employees and subcontractors.

Our broker has confirmed in writing that the cyber liability limit can be raised to $1,000,000 or $2,000,000 within ten business days of a request. Certificates naming the client as additional insured are provided before contract execution.
